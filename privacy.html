Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 4, 2026

1. Overview

Mood to Playlist (“the App”) is designed to help you find music based on a selected mood. The App does not require you to create an account or log in.

2. Information We Collect

The App is designed to operate without collecting personal information from you. We do not ask for your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, precise location, or payment information.

3. Music Platforms

When you select Spotify, YouTube Music, or YouTube, the App opens a search or destination provided by that service. Once you leave the App, the selected platform may collect and process information according to its own privacy policy, terms, cookies, and account settings. Please review the privacy policy of the service you use.

4. Analytics, Advertising and Tracking

The current version of the App is intended to operate without advertising, user tracking, or analytics that identify you. If these technologies are added in a future version, this policy will be updated before or when they are introduced.

5. Children’s Privacy

The App does not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through a service linked from the App, please contact us so we can review the situation.

6. Data Security

Because the App is designed not to collect personal information, there is no user account database maintained by the App. External services you choose to open are responsible for their own security and data practices.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the App’s features or data practices change. The “Last updated” date will be changed accordingly.
